What is Oil Spill Detection?

Oil spill detection involves analyzing satellite images to identify signs of oil on the surface of oceans. Using AI and deep learning models, we can automate this detection to improve response times and reduce environmental damage.

How Our Model Works

We use a pre-trained VGG16 CNN model fine-tuned on a labeled dataset of oil spill vs. clean ocean images. The image is resized, normalized, and passed through the model to classify it.
