About This Project

Combining deep learning with satellite imagery to protect our oceans from environmental disasters.

🌍 Our Mission

Oil spills devastate marine ecosystems, killing wildlife and contaminating water for decades. Early detection is critical. Our AI-powered system analyzes satellite images in seconds, enabling rapid response to minimize environmental damage and protect our oceans for future generations.

Technology Stack

🧠

VGG16 Model

Pre-trained convolutional neural network fine-tuned on thousands of satellite images for high-accuracy oil spill classification.

⚡

TensorFlow

Industry-leading deep learning framework powering our model training, optimization, and inference pipeline.

🌐

Flask

Lightweight Python web framework serving our model predictions through a clean, responsive web interface.

Project Journey

📊 Data Collection

Gathered and curated thousands of labeled satellite images showing oil spills and clean ocean surfaces.

🔬 Model Training

Fine-tuned the VGG16 architecture with transfer learning, achieving 99.2% accuracy on the validation set.

🚀 Web Deployment

Built a Flask web application with drag-and-drop upload, real-time prediction, and a premium user experience.

🌊 Ongoing Impact

Continuously improving detection capabilities and expanding to cover more ocean regions worldwide.
